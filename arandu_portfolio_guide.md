# Arandu Portfolio Design Guide

## Concept

**Name:** Arandu\
**Meaning:** Knowledge / wisdom

**Goal:**\
Create a portfolio that feels like a *place of knowledge and engineering
craft*, not just a list of projects.

**Tone:**\
- Calm\
- Precise\
- Intentional
